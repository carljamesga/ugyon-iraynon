About Ugyon Iraynon

Ugyon Iraynon is a community of outdoor lovers who organize guided hikes across the Philippines. We focus on safety, local culture, and sustainable travel.At Ugyon Iraynon, we believe hiking is about more than just reaching the summit – it’s about the people you meet, the stories you share, and the peaceful moments along the way. We started Trailmates to offer something different: small-group hiking holidays designed for solo travellers who want connection, not crowds

Whether you’re a seasoned trekker or just looking to escape the everyday, our trips are built to feel relaxed, friendly, and deeply rewarding. Each destination is handpicked for its natural beauty, cultural charm, and trail variety – from the wild peaks of the Caucasus to the sun-drenched cliffs of Madeira. We take care of the planning, guiding, and group experience so you can focus on what really matters: the journey.

Our aim is simple – create adventures where strangers become trailmates, and every step brings a little more joy, clarity, and connection. What really sets Trailmates apart is our personal touch. We’re a small, passionate team who genuinely care about your experience – from the moment you book to the final trail view. You won’t find call centres or generic responses here – just real people who love what they do and are here to help. We understand that joining a group trip, especially solo, can feel like a big step. That’s why we keep things relaxed, supportive, and welcoming. Whether you’re a first-time hiker or a seasoned explorer, we’ll make sure you feel comfortable, prepared, and part of the group from day one.
